How large is the GTA VI map?

There are several ways to determine the size of a map.

The most obvious thing to measure is the land area, excluding lakes, rivers and canals. In GTA VI, this is around 82.7 km². Compared to GTA V's 49.7 km², the GTA VI map is about 1.66 times larger.

Alternatively, if we count all terrain above sea level, even if covered by water, we get around 87.2 km². This is 1.70 times more than GTA V's 51.2 km².

Another option is all land and all coastal water within 100 m of the nearest shoreline. This amounts to approximately 113.4 km² in GTA VI, which is about 2.01 times more than GTA V's 56.4 km².

And finally, if we count all modeled terrain, including ocean floor and river beds below sea level, we get an estimate of 187.0 km², or 2.23 times GTA V's 84.0 km².
