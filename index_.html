gtadb, established in 2013, is the premier source for geographic information about Grand Theft Auto. We provide tools, libraries and interfaces that help with the collection, analysis and visualization of in-game data. 100% free, 100% open source.

map.gtadb.org

The original interactive landmarks map for GTA IV, V and VI. Our mission is to match every in-game building to its real-life counterpart.

api.gtadb.org

Lost in Leonida? Not anymore. Try our suite of geo services for GTA VI: elevation, geocoding, landmarks, maps, routes, and more.

software.gtadb.org

Professional tools for mapping open world games from pre-release screenshots and videos, developed for the GTA VI mapping project.

guess.gtadb.org

While we're waiting for the release of GTA VI, this is our first gta6guessr preview round, featuring 25 hard-to-place official screenshots.

files.gtadb.org

A small set of digital artifacts that we have collected over the past 13 years. Mostly related to making maps from large amounts of Snapmatic photos and metadata.
